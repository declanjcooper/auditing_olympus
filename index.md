Welcome to the root node of **Auditing Olympus**. This repository bifurcates the modern technological landscape into two distinct domains: the philosophical satire of the self-appointed gods of the digital age, and the strict, immutable laws of systems architecture.

## 🏛️ The Architecture Wing
Strict architectural documentation and civic defense mechanisms for the modern web.

### The Ostracon Protocol
A procedural framework designed to provide operational accountability against hardware side-channel attacks like FROST. By allowing engineers to challenge unauthorized telemetry collection, the Ostracon Protocol facilitates the submission of structured violation notices to cloud providers, citing breaches of Acceptable Use Policies. Presenting definitive evidence of resource abuse—via raw HTTP headers and parsed payloads—these notices compel network operators to null-route offending infrastructure. It disrupts unauthorized fingerprinting cycles and preserves browser sandbox integrity without waiting for slow standardization processes.
